# Client Intake Checklist

Everything Jacky needs to collect from a client before Claude can build their website + CRM. Works for any vertical. Items marked **[BLOCKING]** must be in hand before the build starts; everything else can arrive during the build.

## 1. Business basics
- **[BLOCKING]** Registered business name + trading name (and SSM registration no. — needed for the contract)
- **[BLOCKING]** Single point of contact: name, phone, email — the ONE person who approves things (avoid design-by-committee)
- Business description: what they do, who their customers are, what makes them different (3–5 sentences in their own words is enough — I'll polish it)
- Year established, team size, service area (which neighbourhoods/cities they serve)

## 2. Branding & content
- **[BLOCKING]** Logo — highest resolution they have (vector/AI/SVG ideal, big PNG acceptable; no logo = flag it, we can generate a simple one)
- Brand colours if they have them (otherwise I'll derive from the logo)
- Photos — the single biggest quality factor for the website:
  - Storefront / premises exterior + interior
  - Team at work
  - Products / services / finished work (before-after shots are gold for service businesses)
  - Minimum ~10 usable photos; phone photos are fine if well-lit
- Any existing marketing copy, brochures, or an old website to pull content from

## 3. Contact, location & hours
- **[BLOCKING]** Address(es) + Google Maps pin
- **[BLOCKING]** Public phone number and WhatsApp number (confirm if same)
- Public email address
- Operating hours, including public-holiday policy
- Social media links (FB / IG / TikTok — whichever they actually use)

## 4. Services & pricing
- **[BLOCKING]** Full list of services/products with a one-line description each
- Price for each — exact, "from RM X", or "ask for quote" (they choose per item; this decides what the website displays publicly)
- Packages, promos, or seasonal offers
- Which services should be **bookable online**, and for each: duration, price/deposit, who can perform it

## 5. Workflow mapping (this configures their CRM pipeline)
Ask them to walk through one real customer, start to finish:
- How does a new customer usually reach them today? (walk-in / WhatsApp / FB / phone / referral)
- What are the steps from first inquiry to job done and paid? → becomes their 6–9 pipeline stages
- Where do leads currently get lost or forgotten? (this is the pain the follow-up automation fixes — remember it for the pitch)
- Staff list: names, roles, who needs a login, who's allowed to see what
- What follow-up do they do today after a job (review request? repeat-visit reminder?) — or wish they did?

## 6. Booking rules (if online booking is in scope)
- Slot length + buffer time between bookings
- Max bookings per day / per staff member
- How far in advance can customers book; minimum notice required
- Cancellation / no-show policy
- Deposit required? Amount and refund terms

## 7. FAQ & communication style
- Top 10 questions customers actually ask (get real examples — becomes the website's FAQ section)
- Who receives website inquiries and booking notifications, and where (name + WhatsApp/email)
- Languages: BM / English / Chinese — which to support, which is primary
- Tone for website copy and follow-up emails (friendly-casual vs formal)

## 8. Accounts & access
- **[BLOCKING]** Domain: do they own one? If yes → registrar login or DNS access. If no → agree the new domain name (client pays domain fee, ~RM 50–100/yr — decide whether this is absorbed or passed through)
- **[BLOCKING]** Google Business Profile — owner or manager access (needed for review automation + pulling live reviews). If they don't have a profile, creating one is part of setup.
- Existing customer list for import: any format (Excel/CSV/phone contacts export) — get whatever they have, I'll clean it
- Existing website hosting details (if migrating away from one)
- Payment collection (if deposits in scope): their bank details for payout / Stripe or local gateway signup — client-owned account, never Jacky's

## 9. Compliance & contract
- **[BLOCKING]** Signed agreement: subscription terms, 1-year lock-in, what "Managed" includes
- PDPA acknowledgment — we store their customers' personal data on their behalf; they remain the data owner
- Confirm who in their company is the data contact for deletion/correction requests

---

## How to run this in practice
- Don't hand the client this whole list — it will scare them. Collect §1–5 in a **single 45–60 min sit-down conversation** (record it / take notes, most of it is just them talking about their business). §6–7 is a second short conversation. §8–9 is homework you chase asynchronously.
- The trial/test-run site can be built from §1–4 alone + placeholder booking rules. Use the trial to create momentum while chasing the rest.
- Photos and Google Business Profile access are the two items clients drag their feet on. Chase early, chase often.
