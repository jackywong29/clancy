# plancy support

plancy is a daily planner for iPhone, made by Clancy Sdn. Bhd., a Malaysian
company (registration no. 202501050621 (1652029-H)). If something
isn't working, or you have an idea, email
[support@clancyhq.com](mailto:support@clancyhq.com). Put the
version number from Settings → About in your message so we know which build
you have.

## Where is my data?

On your iPhone, and nowhere else. plancy has no account and no server, so
Clancy can't see your tasks, journal, ideas or finances, and can't recover
them for you either.

## Moving to a new iPhone

Your plancy data comes with you when you set up the new iPhone from an iCloud
backup, a backup on your computer, or Quick Start. Syncing between two devices
you use at the same time isn't available yet.

## If you delete plancy

Deleting the app deletes its data from your iPhone. Copies in your backups
follow your backup settings.

## Reminders aren't arriving

- Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → plancy, and that notifications are
  allowed.
- Check plancy's Settings → Reminders, and that the task has a time with
  Remind me on. Anytime tasks don't send reminders.
- A Focus mode may be holding them back. Allow plancy in that Focus, or check
  Notification Centre.

## The widget doesn't show task names

That's on purpose while Lock with Face ID covers the whole app: nothing
private appears on the Home Screen or Lock Screen while plancy is locked.

## Face ID

If Face ID doesn't recognise you, plancy offers your iPhone passcode instead.
