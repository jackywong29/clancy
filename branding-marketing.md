# Clancy — brand & marketing playbook

Confirmed 2026-07-07 (Direction C, "porcelain and violet"). Clancy is the working name for both the company and the product — entity registration pending; Jacky will confirm when registered.

## Positioning
Clancy is an AI agency that builds a local business's entire system — website, online bookings, CRM, follow-up automation — in days instead of months, then runs it as a managed service. **The "AI" is in the delivery speed and customization, never a promise of AI features inside the client's product** (in-product AI was removed from scope 2026-07-07 — wording discipline matters in every post and pitch).

- One-liner: **"Built in days. Managed for you."**
- Elevator: "We systemise local businesses."
- Voice: premium-minimal. Plain English, sentence case, no hype words (no "unlock", "seamless", "empower", "game-changer"). Short sentences. Confidence through restraint.

- Flat, whitespace-heavy layouts. No gradients, no drop shadows, no stock photos of handshakes.

## Content system (faceless, English)
Four pillars:
1. **Transformations** — before/after website rebuilds, "WhatsApp chaos → one pipeline" comparisons
2. **Build speed** — screen-recorded timelapses of systems coming together (the proof of "AI agency" without promising AI features)
3. **Systems education** — carousels: where leads die, the stages every business has, what a lost lead costs
4. **Proof** — result stat cards, testimonial text cards, the MegaStar Arena story ("we run a concert arena on this")

Rules:
- **Content is a byproduct of client work, never a separate job.** Record everything while building; each client build yields 5–10 posts. Batch one half-day/month to cut and schedule. Ceiling ~2–4 hrs/week while part-time.
- **Never launch an empty grid** — 9 posts ready before the account goes public (launch grid concept mocked 2026-07-07: before/after · timelapse reel · leads-die carousel · +stat card · wordmark tile · 2am booking reel · tools-replaced tile · scarcity tile · MSA story).
- **Scarcity claims must always be literally true.** "Five clients, never six" style messaging is allowed only while capacity genuinely is five; as the team grows, the line becomes "limited managed slots / waitlist". Never fake scarcity — premium brands get caught.

## Platform playbook
- **Instagram = showroom.** Prospects you pitch will check it; the grid is a portfolio that closes deals. 3 posts/week (1 reel, 1 carousel, 1 static). Highlights: Work / Process / Results / Pricing. CTA: "Book a walkthrough".
- **TikTok = reach engine.** Same reels, rawer cuts, hook-first captions ("This workshop was losing 30 leads a month"). 2–3/week, recycled from IG. Known ceiling: faceless + English-only limits TikTok MY virality — accepted trade for privacy and time.
- **Xiaohongshu = parked.** Mandarin-first platform; revisit only if/when Chinese-language content is added (likely when targeting Chinese-owned workshops/F&B).
