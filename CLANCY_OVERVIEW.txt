1. SNAPSHOT
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
  Clancy is an AI-powered agency and software platform for local businesses in
  Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Each client gets two things built and managed for
  them:
    1. A professional public website (their marketing front end)
    2. An internal CRM / management system (their operational back end)

  It is a genuine multi-tenant SaaS platform — one shared codebase and
  database, every client is an isolated "workspace" — sold as a productized
  monthly subscription with a managed service wrapped around it.

POSITIONING
  "Built in days. Managed for you."
  Premium, personal, small-by-design. The competitive edge is not features —
  it is speed of delivery (AI-assisted builds in days instead of agency
  months) plus a genuinely personal managed service. Clients never touch code
  and never manage the software themselves during their first year.

IMPORTANT WORDING DISCIPLINE
  Clancy is an "AI agency" because AI is used to BUILD and MAINTAIN client
  systems fast. There is deliberately NO AI inside the client product — no
  chatbots, no AI-written replies. This was an explicit decision (7 Jul 2026)
  to remove per-client AI running costs and AI liability. Never market AI
  features inside the client product.

3. THE OFFER — WHAT A CLIENT ACTUALLY GETS
================================================================================

  A) A PUBLIC WEBSITE
     Fully configurable per client, no bespoke code: theme (light/dark),
     custom colours, background image, curated Google fonts with per-text-type
     typography, logo and browser-tab icon, hero, About section, Services with
     prices, photo gallery, location/hours, FAQ, plus unlimited custom
     sections — all reorderable. Booking and contact via WhatsApp deep-link or
     email. A signup form that feeds straight into their CRM.

  B) AN INTERNAL CRM
     - Configurable records: the business decides what a record is called
       (Visitor / Job / Customer / Order) and what fields it holds
     - A pipeline board with their own stages, matching how they actually work
     - Tasks with assignees, due dates, and department-scoped visibility
     - Calendar with colour-coded categories, recurring events, all-day
       events and department alerts
     - People directory (everyone: records from the website, manually added
       records, and staff)
     - Broadcasts — email announcements and newsletters to records or team,
       segmented by stage, role or department
     - Inbox notifications (e.g. new website signups)
     - Team management: three permission levels (view / edit / admin) with
       renameable labels per business, email invites, departments

  C) THE MANAGED SERVICE
     All updates, changes and maintenance handled personally by Jacky during
     the contract. Client requests are batched weekly, not on demand.

4. BUSINESS MODEL
================================================================================

PRICING (confirmed 7 Jul 2026)
  Setup fee ................ RM 0 (free — worth ~RM 5,000 if quoted separately)
  Managed tier ............. RM 1,200 / month
  Self-Serve tier .......... RM 500 / month
  Contract ................. 12-month lock-in
  Pilot variant ............ 50% off year one (RM 600/month) for the first
                             1–2 clients, in exchange for feedback and a
                             testimonial

  Every client starts on Managed for year one (bundled into the lock-in). At
  renewal they either sign another Managed year or drop to Self-Serve — the
  same platform, same data, they just drive it themselves and Jacky stops
  actively managing it. This is a plan/tier flip, NOT an infrastructure
  handover — no export or migration work is required or promised.

  Rationale for the free setup + lock-in pairing: free setup removes the
  buying objection; the 12 months is how the setup investment is earned back.
  Never waive the lock-in — free setup with no lock-in puts all risk on Clancy.

CAPACITY MATH (this is the real constraint, not the software)
  ~3–5 Managed clients per part-time solo founder
  ~15–20 Managed clients per full-time account manager
  Unlimited clients from the platform's perspective — the architecture is
  multi-tenant, so software is never the ceiling. Delivery hours are.

  Marketing must always state the TRUE current capacity. Scarcity is real
  ("limited managed slots") and must never be faked.

5. SALES MOTION
================================================================================

  1. DEMO — show the live MegaStar Arena CRM. Framing: "This runs my venue
     every day. I'm not selling software I read about; I'm selling the system
     I run my own business on."
  2. DISCOVER — open with their pain, not the product. Key questions: How do
     new customers find you? What happens when someone messages at 11pm? How
     do you track a customer from first message to job done? When did a lead
     last disappear?
  3. SKETCH — draw THEIR process as a pipeline on paper. Seeing their own
     business as a pipeline is the hook.
  4. INTAKE — a single 45–60 minute conversation covering their business,
     branding, services, and workflow. (A private link lets the client fill
     the easy parts themselves on their phone.)
  5. TRIAL — build a live site + system in days and hand them the link:
     "It's live. Try booking yourself in."
  6. CLOSE — free setup, RM 1,200/month, 12 months.
  7. ONBOARD — go live within 2–3 weeks of signing; 30-minute handover call
     with their staff.
  8. RENEW — at month 11, renew Managed or move to Self-Serve.

  OBJECTION HANDLING (full scripts in sales-proposal-template.md)
  - "Too expensive" → compare to one admin salary, or to an agency website
    (RM 3–8k) + hosting + booking tool + their own time
  - "What if you disappear?" → your data is yours; after year one you can run
    it self-serve
  - "We use WhatsApp already" → keep it; this catches the customers who don't
    message you — the ones googling at night
  - "A year is long" → that's what makes the RM 5k setup free

6. HOW THE WORK ACTUALLY GETS DONE (the operating leverage)
================================================================================

  This is the part that makes one part-time person able to deliver
  agency-grade work, and it is the thing to protect as the business scales.

  Everything about a client lives in Clancy itself. Two "build briefs" are
  generated automatically from one intake:

      Client intake (one conversation)
                    |
        +-----------+-----------+
        |                       |
   Website brief            CRM brief
        |                       |
   site config             crm config
        |                       |
   their public site      their staff's board

  Jacky copies a brief, pastes it to Claude with an instruction ("build the
  trial for X"), and the system gets built or updated. File uploads (logos,
  photos) travel with the brief as time-limited download links, so the AI can
  fetch real assets.

  Ongoing client requests arrive by WhatsApp, get collected against the client
  record, and are batched weekly into a single instruction. This is why the
  managed promise is deliverable at all.

  THE RULE THAT MAKES SCALE POSSIBLE — "PRODUCT, NOT PROJECTS":
  Every client request must land as reusable configuration or a platform
  feature. Never a bespoke code fork for one client. This single discipline is
  what separates a platform that reaches 150 clients from an agency that dies
  at 20.

7. PLATFORM & TECHNOLOGY
================================================================================

  Stack ............ Next.js 16 (App Router, React Server Components),
                     TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4
  Database/Auth .... Supabase (PostgreSQL) — Singapore region
  Hosting .......... Vercel, auto-deploys from GitHub main branch
  Email ............ Gmail SMTP via nodemailer (transitional; move to a
                     proper email API such as Resend once a real domain is
                     registered)
  Repo ............. github.com/jackywong29/clancy
  Live at .......... clancy-hq.vercel.app

  ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLES
  - True multi-tenant from day one: one shared codebase and database, every
    table scoped by organization_id with row-level security. One org = one
    client. Adding client #100 requires no new infrastructure.
  - Everything per-tenant configurable as DATA, not code: pipeline stages,
    record types, custom fields, departments, roles, website layout — all
    stored as JSON configuration. Changing what a client tracks never
    requires a database migration or a code change.
  - No hardcoded enum constraints in the database (a hard-won lesson from the
    MegaStar CRM, where these repeatedly broke production).
  - Deterministic automations (trigger + template + merge fields), never AI.

  SECURITY POSTURE
  - The wall BETWEEN businesses is enforced at the database layer (RLS) —
    this is the strong boundary and it is solid.
  - Permission levels WITHIN a workspace (view/edit/admin) are currently
    enforced at the application layer. Fine for ordinary SME staff; should be
    hardened to the database layer before any client with genuine
    adversarial-insider risk.
  - Malaysia's PDPA applies — Clancy stores clients' customers' personal data
    on their behalf. Clients remain the data owner.

9. FIVE-YEAR PLAN (targets, not promises)
================================================================================

  YEAR 1 — PROVE IT
    ~5 clients · ~RM 6k MRR · part time
    Build the product, land 2 pilots then 3 full-price clients, launch the
    brand. The metric that matters is renewal and referral, not revenue.

  YEAR 2 — MAKE IT REPEATABLE
    15–20 clients · ~RM 18k MRR · first freelance help
    Second vertical live. Setup effort per client drops from weeks to ~2 days
    as templates mature. Year-one clients hit renewal — first real retention
    data. Product move: LHDN e-invoicing integration (now mandatory for
    Malaysian businesses, badly served by SME tools — a genuine local moat).
    *** THE FULL-TIME GATE SITS HERE. Proposed trigger: 3 consecutive months
    at RM 18k+ MRR with monthly churn under 3%. Jacky must set the real
    number — it has to make leaving a Director's income rational. ***

  YEAR 3 — FULL-TIME AND FIRST TEAM
    40–60 clients · ~RM 50k MRR · 1–2 hires
    Cross the gate, go full time, and hire IMMEDIATELY. Going full time and
    staying solo just moves the ceiling from 5 clients to 15. Hire #1 is an
    account manager; Jacky moves to sales and product.

  YEAR 4 — PLATFORM, NOT AGENCY
    100–150 clients · ~RM 100k MRR · team of 3–5
    Self-serve signup goes public — growth decouples from headcount.
    Payments (FPX), accounting exports, WhatsApp Business API. Referral
    channels open: accountants and marketing agencies reselling Clancy.

  YEAR 5 — CHOOSE THE ENDGAME
    250+ clients · RM 150k+ MRR · team of 6–10
    Three doors, all kept open: a highly profitable lifestyle SaaS owned
    outright, regional expansion (Singapore/Indonesia), or an exit.

10. SCALING LEVERS (ranked by leverage)
================================================================================

  1. LAUNCH THE MARKETING ENGINE — biggest untapped asset. Brand, content and
     assets are all built and sitting unused. Nothing compounds until the
     first post goes out.

  2. GET TO PAID — one signed, paying, referenceable client changes
     everything: it validates pricing, produces a testimonial, generates
     content, and turns the five-year plan from theory into a trajectory.

  3. VERTICAL TEMPLATE PACKS — the fastest route to lower delivery cost.
     Snapshot a completed client's configuration (stages, fields, sections)
     as a named template so the next client in that vertical starts 80%
     pre-configured. Setup time is the whole scaling equation.
     NOTE: the planned verticals were car workshops and F&B, but the first
     real client is a CHURCH. Churches are a genuinely strong vertical —
     recurring events, member retention pipelines, weekly communications,
     tight referral networks between congregations. Worth reconsidering as
     vertical #1 based on actual traction rather than the original plan.

  4. TRACK HOURS PER CLIENT FROM CLIENT #1 — currently not measured. This
     single number drives the full-time gate, the hiring trigger, and whether
     the pricing works. Without it, scaling decisions are guesses.

  5. SELF-SERVE TIER — the structural unlock that decouples growth from
     headcount (planned year 4, could come earlier if demand appears).

  6. REFERRAL CHANNELS — accountants and marketing agencies who already have
     the trust of dozens of local businesses. One good partner outperforms
     months of content.

  7. HIRE AT THE RIGHT MOMENT — an account manager roughly quadruples
     capacity. Hiring late is the classic failure; the hours-per-client log
     is the early warning system.

  8. LOCAL MOAT: LHDN E-INVOICING — mandatory compliance that global SME
     tools handle badly. A real, defensible Malaysian advantage.

  9. PACKAGING EXPANSION — additional sites, extra workspaces, add-on
     modules, annual prepay discounts.

11. RISKS
================================================================================

  CHURN .............. SaaS economics die above ~3–4% monthly churn. Renewals
                       are the year-1 and year-2 obsession.
  THE AGENCY TRAP .... Custom per-client work feels helpful and kills scale.
                       The product-not-projects rule exists for this.
  TWO FULL-TIME JOBS . Years 2–3 danger zone. The revenue gate protects
                       against jumping on hope — and against never jumping.
  KEY-MAN RISK ....... Until operations are documented, Clancy is entirely in
                       Jacky's head. Documentation is insurance.
  HIRING TOO LATE .... Capacity fills silently.
  NO ENTITY YET ...... Contracts should name the company, not Jacky
                       personally. IP ownership and liability ring-fencing.
  MSA ENTANGLEMENT ... Keep infrastructure, books and IP fully separate from
                       MegaStar Arena. (Supabase and GitHub are already
                       separate; the Vercel project still sits under a
                       personal account named "MSA" — move it once the entity
                       exists.)
  PDPA ............... Real obligations once storing clients' customers' data.
  EMAIL DELIVERABILITY  Gmail SMTP is transitional and rate-limited; a proper
                       domain plus an email API is needed before volume.
