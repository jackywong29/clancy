# Clancy — five-year plan

Written 2026-07-07. Numbers are planning targets, not promises — they assume RM 1,200 Managed / RM 500 Self-Serve pricing with the mix shifting toward self-serve over time. The arc in one line: **start as an agency, finish as a platform company.**

| | Theme | Clients | MRR target | Team |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | Prove it | ~5 | RM 6k | Jacky (part-time) |
| Year 2 | Make it repeatable | 15–20 | RM 18k | Jacky + freelance VA |
| Year 3 | Full-time + team | 40–60 | RM 50k | Jacky full-time + 1–2 hires |
| Year 4 | Platform, not agency | 100–150 | RM 100k | 3–5 |
| Year 5 | Choose the endgame | 250+ | RM 150k+ | 6–10 |

## Year 1 — Prove it
Part-time; MegaStar Arena remains the job. Build product phases 1–3, land the 2 pilot clients (50% off year one) then fill to ~5 full-price Managed clients in the workshop vertical, launch the brand with the 9-post grid. The metric that matters is renewal and referral, not revenue. Also: entity registered, contracts under Clancy's name, clean books from ringgit one.

## Year 2 — Make it repeatable
15–20 clients, F&B vertical live, year-1 clients hit renewal (first real retention data). Setup effort per client drops from weeks to ~2 days — templates maturing. First outside help: freelance VA for support/content (not a full hire). Product move: **LHDN e-invoicing integration** — mandatory for Malaysian businesses, badly served by SME tools, genuine local moat.

**The full-time gate sits at end of year 2.** Proposed trigger: 3 consecutive months at RM 18k+ MRR with monthly churn under 3%. **Jacky sets the real number** — it must make leaving the Director role rational. How MSA is managed afterward is Jacky's/the family's decision; the gate just forces that conversation to happen with data.

## Year 3 — Full-time and first team
Cross the gate → full-time → **hire immediately** (going full-time and staying solo just moves the ceiling from 5 clients to 15). Hire #1: account manager — one full-time AM serves ~15–20 Managed accounts; that math replaces the old cap. Jacky moves to sales + product. 40–60 clients; third vertical or double down on the two that work.

## Year 4 — Platform, not agency
Self-serve signup goes public (build plan phase 5) — growth decouples from headcount. 100–150 clients, majority self-serve. Integrations: FPX payments, accounting exports, WhatsApp Business API done properly. Referral channels: accountants and marketing agencies reselling Clancy. If the market pulls for AI features, the archived guardrails in CLAUDE.md apply.

## Year 5 — Choose the endgame
250+ clients, team of 6–10, Jacky is CEO not builder. Three doors, all kept open: profitable lifestyle SaaS owned outright · regional expansion (SG/ID) · exit. No need to choose before arriving.

## Foundations from day one (what makes year 4 possible)
1. **Product, not projects** — every client request becomes reusable config or a platform feature; never a bespoke fork. The rule that separates a platform from an agency that dies at 20 clients.
2. Multi-tenant architecture + billing infrastructure from client #1.
3. **Unit economics logged from client #1**: hours-per-client, churn, MRR. The full-time gate and hire-timing decisions both need this data.
4. Documented operations: intake checklist + sales playbook exist; add an onboarding runbook during the first real onboarding so hire #1 inherits a system, not Jacky's memory.
5. Legal hygiene: Clancy owns the IP, contracts name the company (not Jacky personally), PDPA compliance, zero entanglement with MSA's books.
6. Honest scarcity: capacity claims in marketing update as the team grows.

## Named risks
- **Churn** — SaaS math dies above ~3–4%/month; renewals are the year-1/2 obsession.
- **The agency trap** — custom work per client feels helpful and kills scale; the product-not-projects rule exists for this.
- **Two full-time jobs** — years 2–3 danger zone; the revenue gate protects against jumping on hope, and against burning out by never jumping.
- **Hiring too late** — capacity fills silently; the hours-per-client log is the early warning.
- **Key-man risk** — until SOPs exist, Clancy is Jacky's head; documentation is insurance.
